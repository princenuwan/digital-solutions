FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Some useful information before getting started.

Are these the final prices?

Prices shown are starting prices. The final quotation depends on the features, content, integrations and complexity of your project.

Are subscriptions and third-party fees included?

Domain registrations, hosting, premium software, paid plugins, advertising budgets and third-party subscription charges are generally separate unless explicitly included in your quotation.

Will I own my business accounts?

Wherever supported, accounts should be registered under your business ownership. You can provide access through official role-based permissions.

Can you guarantee Google verification or rankings?

Google and other platforms control their verification, indexing, eligibility and ranking decisions. We cannot guarantee approvals or specific search positions.

How can I request a custom solution?

Send a WhatsApp enquiry describing your business and requirements. We can discuss a suitable solution and provide a custom quotation.
